# Awesome-Engineering-Requirements-Management

## Top Requirements Management (Engineering) Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Systems & Software Requirements, Traceability, Baselines, ALM Integration & Regulated Engineering Workflows*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Requirements Management** in engineering. These tools help teams capture, structure, baseline, review, and trace requirements across the product and systems lifecycle—often with strong links to design, verification, and compliance.

**Examples** include IBM DOORS Next, Jama Connect, Visure Requirements, codebeamer, Polarion ALM, Helix ALM, Azure DevOps, ReqView, Modern Requirements4DevOps, and Valispace (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise requirements management for regulated industries remains heavily commercial. Strong open options include **requirements-as-code** tools (StrictDoc, rmtoo, reqT, Sphinx-Needs), open ALM suites (Tuleap), and related documentation/traceability projects. This section expands those alternatives.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[IBM Engineering Requirements Management DOORS Next](https://www.ibm.com/)**  
  Industry-standard requirements management for complex, regulated systems engineering—baselines, formal change control, and end-to-end traceability.

- **[Jama Connect](https://www.jamasoftware.com/)**  
  Collaborative requirements and test management platform focused on reviews, relationships, live traceability, and product development teams.

- **[Visure Requirements](https://visuresolutions.com/)**  
  Requirements ALM platform for safety-critical and regulated industries with strong compliance and traceability support.

- **[codebeamer (PTC)](https://www.ptc.com/)**  
  Application lifecycle management with integrated requirements, risk, and development workflows for engineering organizations.

- **[Polarion ALM (Siemens)](https://polarion.plm.automation.siemens.com/)**  
  Unified ALM platform covering requirements, quality, and collaboration for product and systems engineering.

- **[Helix ALM (Perforce)](https://www.perforce.com/)**  
  Requirements, test, and issue management suite with traceability across the development lifecycle.

- **[Azure DevOps](https://azure.microsoft.com/services/devops/)**  
  Work item and requirements tracking within Microsoft’s DevOps platform—widely used for agile and hybrid engineering teams.

- **[ReqView](https://www.reqview.com/)**  
  Lightweight yet structured requirements management tool emphasizing offline capability, traceability, and document-oriented workflows.

- **[Modern Requirements4DevOps](https://www.modernrequirements.com/)**  
  Requirements management solution deeply integrated with Azure DevOps for teams already on the Microsoft stack.

- **[Valispace and related MBSE-oriented platforms](https://www.valispace.com/)**  
  Engineering collaboration platforms that connect requirements with system models, parameters, and design data.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[StrictDoc](https://github.com/strictdoc-project/strictdoc)**  
  Open-source requirements-as-code and technical documentation tool—human-readable SDoc/Markdown source with generated views, traceability, and Git-friendly workflows.

- **[rmtoo](https://github.com/rmtoo/rmtoo)**  
  Free and open-source requirements management tool that processes text-file requirements and generates HTML, PDF, graphs, statistics, and quality analytics.

- **[reqT](https://github.com/reqT/reqT)**  
  Open-source requirements engineering tool and modeling language (reqT-lang) for specifying, analyzing, visualizing, and automating requirements—desktop and library form.

- **[Sphinx-Needs](https://github.com/useblocks/sphinx-needs)**  
  Extension for Sphinx that embeds linked “need” objects (requirements, specs, tests) in documentation-as-code workflows with traceability.

- **[Tuleap](https://github.com/Enalean/tuleap)**  
  Open-source application lifecycle management suite with trackers that can be configured for requirements, issues, and related engineering artifacts.

- **[OSRMT and conventional open requirements apps](https://github.com/)**  
  Community open-source requirements management applications offering classic database-backed requirements, tests, and traceability.

- **[Eclipse Capella and MBSE open tooling](https://github.com/eclipse/capella)**  
  Open model-based systems engineering environment that can host and link system requirements within architecture models.

- **[Requirements markup and Git-based open workflows](https://github.com/)**  
  Approaches that treat requirements as version-controlled text/Markdown with review and linking via standard DevOps tools.

- **[Traceability and impact-analysis open helpers](https://github.com/)**  
  Scripts and libraries for linking requirements to tests, design, and code in custom pipelines.

- **[Documentation and requirements-as-code open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  
  Guides for adopting StrictDoc, Sphinx-Needs, rmtoo, or similar tools in regulated or agile engineering contexts.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Adopting **StrictDoc** or **Sphinx-Needs** for requirements-as-code when Git workflows and documentation generation are priorities.
- Using **rmtoo** or **reqT** for structured text-based requirements with analysis and reporting.
- Configuring **Tuleap** trackers as a lightweight open ALM alternative for requirements and related work items.
- Accepting that formal baselines, enterprise-scale concurrent engineering, certified compliance tooling, and deep vendor support for safety-critical domains still favor commercial platforms (DOORS Next, Jama Connect, Polarion, codebeamer, Helix ALM, Visure, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency, version control, and reduced lock-in for engineering teams with strong process ownership.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Author requirements in StrictDoc/Sphinx-Needs/rmtoo → version in Git → generate baselines and documents → link to tests and design artifacts → review via pull requests or open ALM trackers. Suitable for software-heavy and documentation-driven teams. Complex systems and regulated programs often still require commercial requirements ALM platforms.
